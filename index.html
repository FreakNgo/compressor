Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 16, 2026

At Antify Compress (antifycompress.vercel.app), created by Rajveer Nanadikar, your privacy is our absolute priority. This policy explains how your information is handled when using our browser-based file compression tool.

01

100% client-side & local processing

Zero server uploads. When you drop a PNG, JPEG, WebP, or PDF file into the tool, nothing is uploaded to any external server or cloud service.

Local processing. Compression happens entirely inside your web browser using local client-side scripts (browser-image-compression and pdf-lib). Your files never leave your device.

02

Information we do not collect

Because Antify Compress runs purely on your device, we do not collect, monitor, or store file names, file contents, or document metadata; personal data such as names, emails, or contact information; or your saved and compressed output files.

03

Web hosting & external content deliveries

Hosting platforms. The app is served via modern static hosting (such as Vercel or GitHub Pages). Hosting providers automatically log standard server traffic data — like IP address and browser type — strictly for operational stability, performance, and security.

CDNs & fonts. Antify Compress loads Google Fonts (Plus Jakarta Sans, Space Grotesk, JetBrains Mono) and browser compression libraries via secure CDNs (cdn.jsdelivr.net). These deliver JavaScript files directly to your browser to run local compression.

04

Voluntary tips & payment processing

If you use the "Buy me a coffee" feature, payments are handled by opening direct UPI links (upi://pay) to your payment app of choice — Google Pay, Paytm, PhonePe, and similar. All transactions go directly to Rajveer Nanadikar (rajveer-nanadikar@fam). Antify Compress does not collect, process, or store bank details, card information, or UPI credentials.

05

Contact information

Questions about this privacy model or how the app behaves can be sent directly to the developer.
